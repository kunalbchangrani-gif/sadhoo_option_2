Each bottle is an extrait de parfum, so 6–8 hours on skin and 12–16 hours on clothes is typical. It varies with skin, climate and activity.

Start from the feeling. Kailash for stillness, Tripti for warmth, Lavanya for grace, Tattva for grounding. Your free tester lets you try a second.

03What makes Sadhoo different?

Each scent is built around a landscape and a feeling rather than a trend, and every bottle is extrait strength.
